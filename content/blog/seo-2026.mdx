Every year someone declares SEO dead. Every year it quietly keeps sending qualified buyers to businesses that take it seriously. What's changed in 2026 isn't whether SEO works — it's where the traffic shows up and what earns it.

## What changed

AI-generated overviews now sit at the top of many results, answering simple questions before anyone clicks. That's real, and it has trimmed clicks on purely informational queries.

But here's what the panic misses: **high-intent, commercial searches still click through.** People comparing providers, checking prices, or ready to buy don't stop at a summary — they want the source.

## What still moves rankings

- **Genuine topical authority.** Depth beats a scattering of thin posts. Own a topic end to end.
- **Technical health.** Fast pages, clean structure, and solid Core Web Vitals remain table stakes.
- **Real-world signals.** Reviews, mentions, and links from places that matter still carry weight.
- **Search intent match.** The page that best answers *why* someone searched wins — every time.

> The brands winning in search aren't chasing the algorithm. They're building the most useful resource for a query and making it easy to find.

## What to stop doing

Stop publishing shallow content aimed at questions an AI overview already answers for free. Stop obsessing over keyword density. Stop treating links as a numbers game.

## Where to focus this year

Put your effort into commercial and comparison content, structured data so machines understand your pages, and a genuinely fast, well-organized site. That's the compounding kind of work — the traffic it earns keeps paying off long after you publish.

SEO in 2026 rewards the same thing it always has: being the best answer. The tactics evolve; the principle doesn't.
